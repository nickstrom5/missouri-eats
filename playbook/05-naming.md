# Missouri Eats: name, icon and palette (final: Nick's decision 2026-11-07, "Yes, as proposed")

## Name
- **App Store name:** "Missouri Eats: Restaurants" (26). **Subtitle:** "BBQ, Toasted Ravs & Custard" (27). **Home Screen:** "Missouri
  Eats" (13).
- Checked 2026-11-06 with Apple's public search: no app is named "Missouri Eats", "Show-Me Eats" or "Missouri Plates" (results are
  ordering apps, MO Hunting, plate lookups). "Missouri Eats" mirrors Wisconsin Eats and is the plain search fit.
- Alternatives from the prompt: "Show-Me Eats" (the nickname; Show-Me names abound, e.g. "ShowMe STL") and "Missouri Plates" (search
  leans to license plates). Avoided: "MO Eats" (the app "MoEats" exists), VisitMO's "#ThatsMyMO" and "Enjoy the Show", "Missouri Grown".

## Icon
- **A (final, Nick 2026-11-07): two toasted ravioli and a cup of marinara** on flag navy. `scripts/icon-concepts.swift`, `scripts/make-brand.swift`.
- B: a St. Louis-style pizza cut in squares (fails the coverage rule at 38.6% and reads as a waffle). C: burnt ends on butcher paper (passes).
- No Arch (St. Louis only), no team or university marks.

## Palette (the state flag, using the U.S. flag's web values; Missouri sets no hex)
- Navy #0A3161 (12.9:1 on white): titles, tint, accents.
- Red #B31942 (white on it 6.7:1): hand-checked guide cards, focus rings. Deep red #8A1232: honors chips.
- River blue #8DB8DE: fills only (pins, the title shadow), under navy text (6.3:1).
- Limestone cream #F5F1E8: pages.
- Header: the Missouri River, a navy ribbon winding across green bottomland, on a red baseline (Theme.swift `Stripes`, the site's band).
