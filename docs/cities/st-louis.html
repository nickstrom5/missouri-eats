Oldest restaurants in St. Louis

Open since 1960 or before, going by a dated source, listed from the oldest. Each note gives the source of the year. Across Missouri: Missouri's oldest restaurants.

  1. Crown Candy Kitchen

    1401 St Louis Ave, St. Louis

    Since 1913

    A soda fountain and candy kitchen in Old North St. Louis.

    Crown Candy Kitchen's own page: opened in 1913 by Harry Karandzieff and his best friend Pete Jugaloff, in Old North St. Louis, where it stands today.

  2. Ted Drewes Frozen Custard

    4224 S Grand Blvd, St. Louis

    St. Louis classic Since 1931

    The South Grand custard stand.
