Free, with no catch

The app costs nothing, shows no ads and sells nothing inside, and there's no account to make. The lists ship inside the app, so they open right away and keep working when the signal drops out in the Ozarks.

What stays on your phone

Missouri Eats collects nothing about you: no sign-in, no analytics, no ads. Location, if you turn it on, only puts the nearest places first, and your saved places are kept on the phone or iPad. The website has no cookies and no trackers. The privacy policy has the details.

Questions

Is Missouri Eats free?

Completely: no price, no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create. It's an iPhone and iPad app on its way to the App Store, with no Android version yet; until it's out, the web search here works in any browser.

Where do the lists come from?

Three kinds of source. The Kansas City barbecue, toasted ravioli, St. Louis classics, cashew chicken and oldest-places lists are our own research from fall 2026, with a dated 2025 or 2026 source or a Missouri tourism office's own page behind each place. The State of Missouri's active alcohol licenses are public records. The wider directory of 12,765 restaurants starts from open map listings (Overture Maps), filtered to the kinds that proved reliable where states publish restaurant lists.

Where can I get Kansas City barbecue?

Our Kansas City barbecue list has 7 hand-checked joints on the Missouri side, from Arthur Bryant's and Gates on Brooklyn Avenue to Jack Stack at the Freight House. In the web search, the list puts the nearest first once you share your location.

How do I check a Missouri restaurant's inspection?

Missouri's local health departments inspect restaurants and publish their own reports; St. Louis City, St. Charles County and Jefferson County post official grades or scores on their own sites. Springfield-Greene County publishes a weekly inspection feed, so Greene County places matched to it show the department's own result and violation counts, dated; the app computes no grade.

Does it show which places serve alcohol?

Yes, where the State of Missouri's active alcohol licenses show a by-the-drink license at the place's address. With none matched, the app says nothing either way: a club or a new license may be missing.

Does it show ratings and reviews?

Not from us. Tapping a place opens Apple Maps' own card inside the app, with Apple's current ratings, hours, photos and directions. None of our lists is sorted by anyone's rating.

Does it need my location?

No. Location only matters for putting the nearest places first. If you share it, it stays on your iPhone or iPad and never reaches us, and the app works fine without it. On this website, Near me reads your location inside your browser and nowhere else.

A place has closed, or something's wrong. How do I tell you?

Write to nick@eatsranked.com with the place's name, its town and what changed. Corrections ship with the next data update.
