St. Louis classics

8 places for the foods St. Louis made its own, each on Explore St. Louis's signature-foods page, checked in fall 2026.

Slingers, St. Pauls and squares

Explore St. Louis's signature foods start with the slinger at Courtesy Diner and Chili Mac's, the St. Paul sandwich at Mai Lee and Park Chop Suey, and St. Louis-style pizza (cracker-thin, Provel, cut in squares) at Guido's, Farotto's and Monte Bello. Ted Drewes, family-owned for more than 90 years, serves its frozen custard concretes upside down.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: St. Louis (7), Brentwood (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or a Missouri tourism office's own page for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.

On your phone, Missouri Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
