Missouri's oldest restaurants

A tavern, a candy kitchen, a custard stand and a barbecue landmark: Missouri places open at the same address since 1960 or before, 4 places in all, oldest first. The oldest, J Huston Tavern in Arrow Rock, goes back to 1834.

Which year counts

We count from the year a place opened as it stands today, going by a dated source that ties the year to it. A move resets the clock: Stroud's (1933) and Gioia's (1918) aren't dated here until a source ties the year to today's address.

Other guides: Kansas City barbecue, Toasted ravioli, St. Louis classics, Springfield cashew chicken, James Beard honors, Checking an inspection. City pages: St. Louis, Kansas City, Springfield.

Oldest first

  1. J Huston Tavern

    305 Main St, Arrow Rock

    Since 1834

    A tavern on Arrow Rock's Main Street.

    Tasting Table's oldest restaurant in every state (September 2025): Missouri's is the J. Huston Tavern, which got its start in 1834.

  2. Crown Candy Kitchen

    1401 St Louis Ave, St. Louis

    Since 1913

    A soda fountain and candy kitchen in Old North St. Louis.

    Crown Candy Kitchen's own page: opened in 1913 by Harry Karandzieff and his best friend Pete Jugaloff, in Old North St. Louis, where it stands today.

  3. Ted Drewes Frozen Custard

    4224 S Grand Blvd, St. Louis

    St. Louis classic Since 1931

    The South Grand custard stand.

    Ted Drewes' own history: the South Grand store opened in 1931 and is the oldest location still in operation.

  4. Arthur Bryant's Barbecue

    1727 Brooklyn Ave, Kansas City

    KC barbecue Since 1958

    The Brooklyn Avenue original by 18th and Vine; Visit KC credits it with popularizing burnt ends.

    Arthur Bryant's own history: it moved to its present location at 1727 Brooklyn Avenue in 1958.
